# Quick Audit Grading Skill

## Read first

Load `references/source-docs/quick-audit-grading-approach-v1.md` before grading — it holds the full
anchored-reasoning method, the letter bands, the hard-cap floors, and the Apparel Junction (D 38:
42/55/38/40/35/18) and RiteWay (A ~90) calibration anchors. Do not grade from memory.

## Inputs

- Validated intake context.
- Enrichment record.
- `evals/anchored-grading-rubric.md`.

## Process per pillar

1. Enumerate 5-10 concrete signals.
2. Select an anchor: Apparel Junction D, RiteWay A, or verbal band if neither anchor fits.
3. Assign letter and 0-100 score by judgment.
4. Write a 3-5 sentence rationale.
5. Record the result in Grading Notes.

## Pillars

- Footprint & Listings
- Reviews & Reputation
- Social Media
- Website Performance
- Brand Search
- Keyword Rankings

## Output

- Six pillar grades.
- Overall rounded average grade.
- Grading Notes markdown.
- Hard caps and data unavailable log.
