# Anchored Grading Rubric

## Purpose

Evaluate whether the grading output follows the anchored-reasoning method.

## Required evidence per pillar

- 5-10 concrete signals.
- Named anchor or explicit note that neither anchor fits.
- Letter grade.
- 0-100 score.
- 3-5 sentence rationale.
- Data unavailable note when applicable.

## Hard caps

- No GBP found or no claimed Google listing: Footprint capped at F; Brand Search capped at D.
- Website unreachable or SSL broken: Website Performance and Keyword Rankings capped at F.
- No reviews on any platform: Reviews capped at F.
- Zero ranking keywords and zero referring domains: Keyword Rankings capped at F.
- No social channels of any kind: Social Media capped at F.

## Pass criteria

The output passes only if every pillar includes visible signals, an anchor/rationale, and defensible grade placement.
